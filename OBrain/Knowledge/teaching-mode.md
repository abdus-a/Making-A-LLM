## How to ask understanding-checks (learned 2026-07-30, Sxn_0003)
Abstract questions don't land. When checking understanding:
- **Lay the actual numbers out** in front of him — don't ask him to hold a setup in his head.
- **One question at a time**, not a list of three.
- **Always offer the exit:** "or I can just walk you through it" — the goal is understanding,
  not passing a quiz.
